## Brand & Style

This design system blends traditional Japanese spatial aesthetics—specifically the concept of *Ma* (間, negative space and rhythmic pause)—with the functional clarity of Scandinavian craft minimalism (Japandi). Designed for curated lifestyle workshops, craft ateliers, and cultural short-courses, the interface prioritizes stillness, mindful tactile warmth, and organic balance over dense utility.

### Core Tenets
- **Ma (間) & Airiness:** Uncluttered layouts, generous margins, and deliberate emptiness allow each craft, ceramic, or tea workshop to feel like an exhibit in a serene physical gallery.
- **Wabi Tactility & Warm Earth:** Textures and palettes evocative of hand-turned terracotta, fired stoneware, raw cedar wood, and light warm washi paper create grounded elegance without synthetic harshness.
- **Intentional Restraint:** Visual hierarchy is communicated through refined typography, muted natural contrasts, and organic balance rather than aggressive saturation.

## Colors

The palette transitions the brand's focal chromatic identity into warm Japanese terracotta and steeped tea wood tones (`#8C5E43`), balancing natural earthen vitality with tranquil paper surfaces.

- **Primary (`#8C5E43` - Terracotta / Tea Wood):** The core active color, applied across primary interactive buttons, prominent tags, focal states, and critical brand touchpoints. On hover and active engagement, it transitions to a deeper earthen wood tone (`#724B35`).
- **Primary Container & Tonal Shifts:** Surfaces requiring soft terracotta warmth utilize container tiers (`#F2EAE5`) paired with deep terracotta text (`#452B1E`) for gentle, low-friction hierarchy.
- **Secondary (`#705A44` - Warm Cedar):** Provides grounded support for auxiliary metadata, secondary badges, dates, and subtle borders.
- **Tertiary (`#5C3F44` - Dried Plum / Sumi Rosé):** Reserved for celebratory milestones, seasonal limited courses, and distinct workshop highlights.
- **Canvas & Paper Foundations:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FCF9F8`): Clean, warm washi paper that eliminates stark sterile whites.
  - Surface Container Low (`#F6F3F2`): Subdued container tone for section grouping and contextual modules.
  - Workshop Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`): Pristine warm white reserved strictly for elevated interactive cards and dialog panels.
- **Typography & Structure:**
  - Charcoal Ink (`#1B1C1C`): Softened, non-pure black that preserves editorial warmth.
  - Stone Ash (`#434843`): Supporting metadata, course duration, and location signifiers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs literary Japanese serif headlines with clear, rational sans-serif body and transactional data.

- **Headlines & Editorial Display:** Set in `Noto Serif`. Spacious line heights grant workshop titles and narrative descriptions an unhurried, bookish character.
- **Body & Captions:** Set in `Noto Sans` for effortless legibility across materials, techniques, and studio disclaimers.
- **Labels & Interactive Elements:** Action buttons, session filters, and price tags utilize `Noto Sans` with slightly tracked letter spacing (0.06em to 0.08em) to ensure instantaneous legibility even at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an unhurried 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width 1280px), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Breathing Space:** Section dividers and component groups must utilize `space-xl` (3rem) to preserve the feeling of serene physical galleries.
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Canvas boundaries are cushioned by wide outer margins (`3rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile) to prevent interface density.
- **Visual Media Discipline:** Hero displays and card imagery maintain clean 16:9 or 4:3 ratios, framed by consistent gutters to respect the photographic subject matter.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on delicate tonal paper tiers and diffuse, warm-tinted ambient shadows rather than synthetic gray drop-shadows.

- **Ambient Warm Shadow:** Floating cards and interactive surfaces use an ultra-soft amber-gray shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(140, 94, 67, 0.06)`. On hover, workshop cards rise softly with `0 14px 40px rgba(140, 94, 67, 0.10)`.
- **Surface Tiering:** The baseline canvas rests on `#FCF9F8`. Subordinate grouping panels sit on `#F6F3F2`. Floating interactive workshop cards rest on pure `#FFFFFF` to focus attention effortlessly.
- **Hairline Framing:** In lieu of heavy boundaries, panels and dividers use soft 1px strokes colored with `rgba(27, 28, 28, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape geometry maintains measured architectural calm:

- **Cards, Sheets & Modals:** Standardized with an 8px radius (`0.5rem`, level 2). This delivers tailored craftsmanship without clinical hardness or overly playful curves.
- **Action Pills & Badges:** Interactive filter chips, workshop duration indicators, and category tags adopt full pill shapes (`9999px`) to create smooth ergonomic touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) background with crisp white typography, 8px border radius, 48px standard height, and 0.06em tracked label typography. Transitions smoothly to `#724B35` on hover with a 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a 1px border of `rgba(140, 94, 67, 0.25)` and Charcoal Ink text. Transitions to Terracotta Light (`#F2EAE5`) on hover.
- **Text Link CTA:** Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) with a delicate 1px underline offset by 4px.

### Chips & Filter Tags (Capsules)
- **Geometry:** Height of 32px or 36px, with full pill curvature (`9999px`).
- **Unselected:** Warm white surface (`#FFFFFF`), subtle Stone Ash text, and hairline warm border.
- **Selected:** Solid Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) with crisp white typography.

### Workshop Cards
- **Structure:** 16:9 photography header over generous internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- **Surface:** Pure warm white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warm shadow.
- **Details:** Terracotta pill tag for category (e.g., "Wood Turning", "Tea Ceremony", "Ceramics"), `headline-md` title, and tabular price/schedule row at the base.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white background, 8px radius, height 48px, hairline border `rgba(27, 28, 28, 0.12)`.
- **Focus State:** Border sharpens to Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) supported by an ambient outer ring glow `rgba(140, 94, 67, 0.15)`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px with a 4px soft radius. Checked state fills with Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) displaying a clean white glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circle filling with Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`) when selected.

### Curated Timeline & Milestone Nodes
- **Step Indicators:** Vertical and horizontal workshop timelines feature minimal 6px node dots and 1px track lines rendered in Terracotta Wood (`#8C5E43`).
